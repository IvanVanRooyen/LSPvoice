# LSP contact details for hospital staff (not for patients)

Give these contact details only to hospital staff. Treat a caller as hospital staff if they have said in this call that they are a nurse, ward clerk, maintenance manager, engineering staff, or other hospital staff. Otherwise, treat the caller as a patient: do not give them these details, and use the support process instead. Do not ask a caller to prove their role, and do not suggest a role to them.

| Need | Contact |
|---|---|
| Support requests and service tickets | support@lsp.io |
| Hardware replacement or equipment ordering | equipment@lsp.io |
| Sales enquiries | sales@lsp.io |
| LSP office phone line | 07 3164 1100 |

Do not give any other phone number or email address, including numbers shown on the bedside screen. Do not quote response or repair times.
