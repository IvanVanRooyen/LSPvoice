# Buying entertainment on the bedside screen

Applies to bedside screens with a **Click to Pay** panel on the right of the home screen. If the caller's screen looks different, ask what they can see and offer approved support. Do not invent buttons or steps.

## Rules for prices, payment steps and help details

The payment system is still being finalised, so the patient's screen is the authority:
- Prices are in the separate entertainment prices guide. If the caller's screen shows something different, the screen is correct. Never offer a discount.
- For payment, say "follow the steps on your screen" and help the caller understand what they are reading. Never ask for, repeat or accept card or bank details.
- If the screen shows something unexpected, or text that looks unfinished, don't explain it away. Offer the approved support process.
- Information on the screen does not confirm payment, entitlement, concession eligibility or activation. Use the assistance flow and the backend result for those.

## If entertainment has run out or was never bought

- The panel on the right of the home screen shows **EXPIRED** with the time and date it ended.
- In **ENTERTAINMENT**, a bar at the top says **No active package**. Tapping a channel, radio station or relaxation item opens the package list instead of playing. This is expected, not a fault.

## Buying a package

1. On the home screen, tap **Click to Pay** in the panel on the right.
2. On **Your Entertainment**, tap **Buy a Package**. From ENTERTAINMENT, the caller can instead tap **View Packages**, or any channel.
3. The package list shows the options and their prices. Ask the caller which one they want, then have them tap **Order** on it.
   - If the caller says they have a concession, the **Concession Holders** button at the bottom right shows the concession list. Do not tell them whether they qualify.
   - **Terms of Service** is at the bottom right of the screen.
4. On **Select Payment Method**, the caller chooses **Card Payment**, **QR Pay** or **Use Voucher**, then follows the steps on the screen.
   - **QR Pay:** scan the code with a phone camera and finish paying on the phone.
   - **Use Voucher:** type the voucher code using the on-screen keypad, then tap **Continue**.
   - **Card Payment:** the screen shows the amount due. Ask the caller to check it matches the package they chose before continuing.

## Going back

On the payment screens, the curved arrow at the top left can return to the **home screen** and the order has to be started again. That is not a fault: start again from **Click to Pay**.

## Past purchases and time left

- **Click to Pay** then **My Purchases** opens **History**, a list of past payments and packages with dates.
- Time left is shown in the home screen panel and at the bottom of the **Your Entertainment** screen.

## Help and identifying the screen

- A **question-mark icon** at the top right of the home screen opens the help screen.
- The bed name is at the top centre of the home screen. A **Device ID** is shown at the bottom of the purchase and help screens. Ask for these to identify the screen.
