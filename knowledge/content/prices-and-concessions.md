# Entertainment prices shown on the bedside screen

These are the prices shown on the LSPcare 15.6-inch bedside terminal (LSPcare app 6.0) on 24 November 2026. Prices can change and may differ between hospitals. If the caller's screen shows a different price, the screen is correct: ask them to read it out, and do not insist on these figures.

| Package | Length shown on screen | Public patient | Concession holder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Day | 24 hours | $10.00 | $9.00 |
| 2 Days | 48 hours | $19.00 | $17.00 |
| 3 Days | 72 hours | $27.50 | $24.00 |
| 4 Days | 96 hours | $36.00 | $36.00 |
| 5 Days | 120 hours | $43.00 | $36.00 |
| 1 Week | 144 hours | $54.00 | $47.00 |

- Concession prices are shown when the caller taps **Concession Holders** at the bottom right of the package list. Concession prices are for concession card holders. Never confirm whether a caller qualifies.
- Before paying by card, the screen shows the amount due. Ask the caller to check that it matches the package they chose.
- Never invent a discount, exception or refund. Never collect card or bank details in the support conversation. If the caller disputes a charge or a price, use the approved support process.
