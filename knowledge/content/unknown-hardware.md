# Troubleshooting when the hardware setup is unknown

Reuse the hospital, bed and device details already provided. Ask what the caller can see and record their description without treating appearance as proof of network, payment or activation state.

Only give physical troubleshooting instructions approved for the identified setup. Without a verified profile, do not assume a power/status light, remote control, Home button, TV input selector, accessible wall socket or safe restart procedure. Do not ask the caller to move or unplug equipment based on a generic guess.

If a feature is confirmed absent for the identified profile, say that it is absent and use that profile's approved alternative. If a capability is unknown, clarify or offer support. Caller corrections about their equipment must be respected.

A black screen is an observation, not a diagnosis of the cable, power supply or network. With no approved setup-specific steps, offer the support process. Do not promise a technician visit, successful transfer or callback until the relevant support action is confirmed.

A clinical request immediately uses the nurse-call/ward-staff redirect and ends the support conversation; do not continue equipment troubleshooting.
