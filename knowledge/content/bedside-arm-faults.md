# Bedside screen arm: loose, dropping or not holding position

The bedside screen is held by a wall-mounted arm. LSPcare hospitals use several arm models: a 1200 mm arm, a 1600 mm arm and a 1982 mm arm (the number is how far the arm reaches). Which arm is fitted at a particular bed is not recorded yet. Do not guess or tell the caller which model they have. The advice below is the same for every arm.

If the caller says the arm or screen is dropping, sagging, drifting, swinging, too stiff, too loose, won't stay where it is put, or a cover on the arm has come off:

- Say: "Please don't try to adjust it. We'll arrange support." Then use the support process.
- Do not troubleshoot the arm. Never explain how to adjust, tighten or loosen the arm, its joints, tilt, spring tension, screws, covers or the cables inside it, even if the caller has tools, names the arm model or has read an installation guide. Arm adjustments are made only by an LSP technician or installer.
- Do not ask the caller to hold, push, lift, move or take apart the arm or the wall bracket, and do not ask them to touch any cable.
- Do not say how long the repair will take or when someone will come. Do not promise a visit until the support action is confirmed.

If anyone has been hurt, or the caller needs help from a nurse, use the nurse-call/ward-staff redirect straight away and stop the equipment conversation.
