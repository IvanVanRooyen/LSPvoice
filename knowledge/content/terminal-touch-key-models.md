# 21-inch bedside terminals (touch symbols along the bottom, no orange button)

LSPcare's 21-inch bedside terminals have **no orange button**. Instead they have a row of small touch symbols printed on the glass along the bottom edge, below the picture. There are two versions, and they work the same way apart from these symbols and their buttons. Recognise them from what the caller describes. The hospital bed does not need to be known first.

If the caller describes an orange button in the middle of the frame below the screen, use the LSPC15mf guide instead. If the description fits neither version, or the caller isn't sure, use the unknown-hardware guidance. These terminals do not come with a telephone handset or remote control.

## Version A: black glass front, eight symbols

About eight touch symbols run along the bottom of the black glass: a **light bulb** at the far left, then a curved **back** arrow, a speaker with one wave (**volume down**), a speaker with more waves (**volume up**), a **house**, a **down** arrow, an **up** arrow, and a **power** symbol at the far right. This version has no physical buttons.

- **Screen off or black:** touch the **power symbol** at the far right of the row once, with a short touch. It turns the screen on or off.

## Version B: white frame, "LSPcare" at the bottom left, nine symbols

Nine touch symbols are in a row in the middle of the bottom edge: a **light bulb**, a **house**, a **back** arrow, a **down** arrow, an **up** arrow, a **small screen**, a speaker with a minus sign (**volume down**), a speaker with a plus sign (**volume up**), and a **sun**.

- **Screen off or black:** touch the **small screen symbol** once. It turns the screen off or wakes it.
- **Sun symbol:** adjusts screen brightness.
- There is also a round power button at the bottom right of the back of the terminal. Do not ask the caller to use it. Offer the support process instead.

## Both versions

- **Light bulb symbol:** switches the reading lamp on or off. Patients may use it. It is not a power or status light, so never treat it as a sign that the terminal is on or working.
- **Volume symbols:** patients may use them.
- **Headphones:** wired headphones plug into the socket at the back of the terminal, in the middle, just below where the arm attaches. Bluetooth headphones also work. Headphones are not supplied by LSP or the hospital, so the patient needs their own.
- **Connecting Bluetooth headphones:** patients can do this themselves. On the home screen, open the Settings or **APPS** icon, then the **Bluetooth** icon. Make sure the Bluetooth switch is turned on, then select **Scan & clear paired devices** at the top right and follow the steps on the screen. If the caller can't find these icons, don't guess. Offer the support process.
- Never use the LSPC15mf (orange button) steps on these terminals.

## Black screen on a 21-inch terminal

1. If the caller needs a nurse or any clinical help, use the nurse-call/ward-staff redirect first and stop here.
2. Ask: "Is the screen completely black, or can you see anything on it?" If something is showing, help with what they can see.
3. Confirm the version from the symbols the caller describes, then ask them to touch the power symbol once (version A) or the small screen symbol once (version B). Ask them to wait a few seconds.
4. If the screen comes back on, the problem is solved.
5. If it stays black, do not ask them to touch it again repeatedly, use the round button on the back, unplug anything or move the arm. Offer the support process.

If the caller cannot reach the screen comfortably, do not ask them to stretch or move. Offer the support process.
